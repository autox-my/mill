# Slice S-02 Plan: Protect Human Attention (`merge_risk` + Verification Skills)

## Goal
Protect scarce human review attention by adding a persistent `risk` (`low` | `medium` | `high`) field on `domain.Slice` with an un-gameable deterministic floor computed from run telemetry, surfacing risk badges and risk-sorted `Ready for human` cards in `internal/webui`, and adding `merge-risk` and `ui-verification` repo skills under `.agents/skills/`.

## Scope
1. **Domain (`internal/domain`):** `RiskLevel` (`low`, `medium`, `high`), validation, ordering, and deterministic risk floor (`FootprintFindings > 0` or `ReqDiffDirection` in `breaking`/`loosening` → `high`; `Amendments > 0` or `Verdict` in `RED`/`UNDECIDABLE` → `medium`; otherwise `low`).
2. **Store (`internal/store`):** Schema column `risk TEXT NOT NULL DEFAULT 'low'`, idempotent `ALTER TABLE` migration for existing SQLite DBs, and deterministic floor enforcement in `RecordRun` and `UpdateSliceState`.
3. **Adapters & CLI (`httpapi`, `httpclient`, `mcp`, `cmd/mill`):** Expose and round-trip `risk` on slice updates and listings.
4. **Web UI (`internal/webui`):** Render `risk` badges on slice cards, highlight `high`-risk cards, sort `Ready for human` descending by risk (`high` → `medium` → `low`), and display `Merge risk` in the slice detail drawer.
5. **Skills & Claim:** Add `.agents/skills/merge-risk/SKILL.md`, `.agents/skills/ui-verification/SKILL.md`, and `claims/REQ-0012.yml`.
